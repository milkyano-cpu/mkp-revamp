How it works

Tell us about your bike

Send the make, model, size, groupset and a few photos, or simply bring it into our Cheltenham showroom.

We inspect it

Frame condition, drivetrain wear, wheels, bearings and service history are checked before we make an offer.

Sell or trade-in

Take payment by bank transfer, or use the value as a trade-in towards your next bike.

Get an offer

Fill this in and we’ll open a WhatsApp chat with your details ready to send. Attach a few photos there and we’ll come back with an offer.

What we buy

Quality road, gravel and triathlon bikes. Recent-generation bikes with Shimano 105, Ultegra or Dura-Ace, or SRAM Rival and higher, are the strongest candidates.

Proof of ownership is required for every bike we buy.
