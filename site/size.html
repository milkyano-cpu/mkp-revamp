How to measure yourself accurately

Measuring height
  1. Stand barefoot with your back against a wall
  2. Keep your feet flat on the ground
  3. Look straight ahead (not up or down)
  4. Place a book or flat object on top of your head
  5. Mark the position and measure from the floor
Measuring inseam
  1. Stand barefoot with your back against a wall
  2. Place a book between your legs, firmly against your crotch
  3. Keep the book level and mark where it touches the wall
  4. Measure from the floor to this mark
  5. This is your inseam length
