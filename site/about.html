About MKP Bikes.

Welcome to MKP Bikes, Cheltenham's trusted destination for premium refurbished road and gravel bikes. Each bike is professionally inspected, fully serviced, and ready to ride, ensuring performance and reliability you can feel from your first pedal stroke.

Whether you're a seasoned cyclist or just starting out, discover exceptional value across a handpicked range of second-hand bikes from leading brands. Visit our Cheltenham VIC showroom and experience the MKP difference: expert service, honest advice, and bikes that inspire confidence.

How it started

Quality cycling shouldn't break the bank.

MKP Bikes was founded with a simple belief: quality cycling shouldn't break the bank. We saw too many perfectly good bikes being overlooked, and too many riders missing out on the joy of cycling because of cost barriers.

Our approach is straightforward: we source premium road and gravel bikes, put them through comprehensive professional servicing, and offer them at prices that make sense. No compromises on quality, no hidden issues, just honest bikes ready for their next adventure.

Today, we're proud to serve Melbourne's cycling community from our Cheltenham showroom, helping riders find their perfect bike and keeping the cycling community thriving.
