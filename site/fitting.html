Is it for you?

If any of these sound familiar.

Pain

Knee, back or neck pain

Discomfort is often caused by poor positioning, not your body. A proper fit removes most of it.

Numbness

Numb hands or feet

Usually pressure points from incorrect reach, saddle position or cleat alignment.

Power

Feel sluggish pedalling

Working hard but not going fast? Your position may be costing you watts.

New bike

Start right

A fit from day one prevents bad habits, avoids injury and sets the bike up for your body.

Comeback

Returning from injury

Your body changes. A re-fit accounts for flexibility, strength and mobility.

Racing

Chasing marginal gains

More power, more comfort, and longer rides without fatigue.

How it works

Five steps to your best position.

01

Conversation

Riding history, goals, current discomfort and what you want from the fit.

02

Body assessment

Functional screening of flexibility, mobility and asymmetries.

03

On-bike assessment

Biomechanical angles, movement patterns and pedalling dynamics.

04

Adjustments

Real-time changes to saddle, reach, bars and cleats based on the data.

05

Fit report

A detailed report of your final position and next steps.
